RPE Guide

RPE means Rate of Perceived Exertion. It tells you how hard the set felt based on how many good reps you had left.

Progression Rules

Add weight when you hit the top of the rep range with clean form and the target RPE.
Keep weight the same when form was good but reps were not at the top yet.
Lower weight if you miss the rep range, form breaks, or the set feels like RPE 9 or 10.
Skip optional cardio when legs feel beat up, sleep is poor, or recovery is low.

Session Structure

Warm up: 5 to 8 minutes easy cardio plus warm up sets.
Main work: Complete the listed lifts with controlled reps.
Optional split: Lift for 45 minutes, then do cardio later for 20 to 35 minutes.
Cool down: 5 minutes easy walking and light mobility.
