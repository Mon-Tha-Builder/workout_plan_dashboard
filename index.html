RPE Guide

RPE means Rate of Perceived Exertion. It tells you how hard the set felt based on how many good reps you had left.

Progression Rules

Add weight when you hit the top of the rep range with clean form and target RPE.

Keep weight the same when form was good but you are not at the top of the rep range yet.

Lower weight if form breaks, pain shows up, or the set feels like RPE 9 to 10.

Skip optional cardio when recovery is low or legs feel beat up.
